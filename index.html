Why Sidhpur?

Where our story began

For many Ismaili families in America, the road home leads to the villages of North Gujarat — the Sidhpur, Patan, and Mehsana region where our community's roots run centuries deep. This is the land where early Pirs first shared the faith, where our great-grandparents farmed, traded, and built jamatkhanas, and from where families set out for East Africa, Karachi, and eventually America.

Sidhpur itself is one of Gujarat's most beautiful heritage towns — famous for its pastel-colored historic havelis, the thousand-year-old Rudra Mahalaya, and the sacred Bindu Sarovar lake. It sits on the banks of the Saraswati River, about 2.5 hours north of Ahmedabad — surrounded by the very villages so many of our families come from.

December is the perfect month to visit: cool mornings, sunny days around 75°F, and festival season in full swing.

  • Bohra Vad heritage streetsRows of ornate century-old mansions — one of India's most photographed heritage quarters.
  • Historic Methan JamatkhanaThe village where Mawlana Hazar Imam laid a jamatkhana foundation in 1978 — visit and learn its history.
  • Rani ki Vav, PatanA UNESCO World Heritage stepwell 30 minutes away — plus Patan's famous Patola silk weavers.
  • Our ancestral villagesDozens of villages with historic jamats and jamatkhanas surround Sidhpur.
  • AKDN at workSee Aga Khan Development Network projects serving rural Gujarat firsthand.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What is Impact Festival?

A 4-day, not-for-profit community celebration held December 24–27, 2026 in Sidhpur, Gujarat — bringing Ismaili families from across the USA back to the region where our community's roots began, together with the local jamat, for food, music, heritage tours, and community impact.

When exactly is it?

December 24–27, 2026 — these dates are tentative and being confirmed. Four days of festival, Thursday Dec 24 through Sunday Dec 27. Most guests will arrive a day early and leave a day after. Sign up above and we'll notify you the moment dates are locked in, before you book flights.

What's included?

The festival program covers your meals and all festival venues and events — the dinners, dandiya nights, concert, mela, and organized programs. Details on the full package will be shared with registered guests.

Are flights included?

No — guests arrange their own flights to India. Most travelers fly into Ahmedabad (AMD), about 2.5 hours from Sidhpur by road. Book early: late December is peak holiday season for India travel, so fares rise sharply and seats sell out months ahead. We'll share travel guidance and group-coordination options as the event approaches.

Where will we stay?

We're arranging block hotel bookings, host-family stays with local Ismaili homes, and other options at a range of comfort levels. Accommodation details and pricing will be shared with registered guests.

Do I need a visa?

US passport holders need an Indian visa or e-Visa (OCI cardholders don't). The e-Tourist Visa is a simple online application — we'll send step-by-step guidance to everyone who signs up. Apply early!

What's the weather like in December?

Beautiful — North Gujarat in December means sunny days around 75–82°F and cool, pleasant evenings around 55°F. Bring a light jacket for the nights.

Is this family-friendly? What about elders?

Absolutely — this is designed as a multigenerational trip. There's a dedicated youth camp, and we're planning accessible transportation and seating for seniors. Tell us about your group's needs in the sign-up form.

Will the food be halal / vegetarian-friendly?

Yes. All festival meals will be halal, with abundant vegetarian options — this is Gujarat, after all, home of India's best vegetarian food.

Can relatives from outside the USA join?

Yes! Family joining from India, Canada, or anywhere else is welcome. Note it in your sign-up and we'll plan accordingly.

I don't know my family's village — can I still find my roots?

Yes — many families have rediscovered their ancestral villages through elders and local jamat records. Tell us what you know and our local team will help trace it before the trip.

Who's organizing this?

A volunteer team from our community, working with local jamati leaders in Gujarat. For any questions, contact the Impact Festival Team — impactfestival26@gmail.com.
